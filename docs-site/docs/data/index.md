# Data

Find airborne and satellite hyperspectral products and bring provider files into hyperproc's shared `xarray` interface. Follow the path that matches where you are starting.

## From archive to dataset

<div class="entry-steps" markdown>

1. **Find a scene**

    Search NASA CMR, NEON, or DLR by location and date. If your files are already downloaded, go straight to reading a product.

    [Search and download →](../workflows/search.md) · [Check access and delivery formats →](../getting-started/data-access.md)

2. **Check sensor support**

    Confirm the supported product level, required files, geometry, and reader limitations before opening a delivery.

    [Compare supported sensors →](../sensors/index.md)

3. **Read and inspect**

    Open a local provider product and inspect its measurements, wavelengths, geometry, and quality flags.

    [Open your first dataset →](../getting-started/quickstart.md) · [Read and inspect workflow →](../workflows/reading.md)

</div>
